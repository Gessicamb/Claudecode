## Example Carousel: "5 Morning Habits That Changed My Productivity"

**SLIDE 1 (Hook):**
I wasted 3 years on morning routines that didn't work.
Then I found these 5 habits — my output doubled in 60 days. Here's exactly what changed 👇

**SLIDE 2:**
Habit 1: No phone for the first 30 minutes
Most people start their day reacting to others' agendas. Your first 30 minutes set the tone for your entire day. Begin with your own intentions — not someone else's notifications. This single change reduces morning anxiety by up to 40% according to behavioral studies.

**SLIDE 3:**
Habit 2: Write 3 priorities the night before
Decision fatigue is real. By morning, your willpower is at its peak — don't waste it deciding what to do. Write tomorrow's 3 non-negotiables before bed. You wake up with a clear mission instead of a blank slate.

**SLIDE 4:**
Habit 3: Move your body within 60 minutes of waking
Exercise before checking email changes your neurochemistry. Cortisol drops. Dopamine rises. Creativity follows. Even a 15-minute walk counts. The goal is activation, not a full workout.
