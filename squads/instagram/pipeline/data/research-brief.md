# Research Brief — Instagram Squad

## Platform Overview
Instagram is a visual-first platform with 2B+ monthly active users. Content is consumed quickly — users decide in 1-2 seconds whether to engage. Carousels receive ~1.4x more reach than single images.

## Engagement Hierarchy
Saves > Shares > Comments > Likes (in order of algorithmic weight)

## Content That Performs
- Educational carousels (tips, lists, how-tos) — high save rate
- Storytelling posts (personal journey, transformation) — high comment rate
- Contrarian/surprising facts — high share rate
- Behind-the-scenes content — high comment rate

## Algorithm Signals
- Early engagement (first 30-60 min) is critical for distribution
- Carousels get a second chance at impressions (re-served if user didn't swipe)
- Consistent posting (4-7x/week) maintains algorithmic favor
- Shares to DMs are the highest-value engagement signal

## Caption Best Practices
- First 125 chars must hook the reader (visible without "more")
- 800-1200 character captions outperform both very short and very long
- 8-15 hashtags is the optimal range (30 is the hard limit)
- End with a low-friction CTA ("Save this for later", "Tag someone who needs this")

## Posting Windows
- Best: 9-11 AM or 7-9 PM in audience's local timezone
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Avoid: Monday morning, Friday evening, weekends (unless lifestyle brand)
