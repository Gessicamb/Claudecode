# Tone of Voice — Instagram Content

## Available Tones

### 1. Educational
**Voice:** Clear, structured, authoritative but approachable.
**When to use:** How-to posts, tips lists, explanatory carousels.
**Example opening:** "Here's why most people get X wrong — and the 3-step fix."
**Avoid:** Jargon, condescension, overly academic language.

### 2. Inspirational
**Voice:** Warm, motivating, emotionally resonant.
**When to use:** Personal transformation, mindset posts, milestone content.
**Example opening:** "One year ago I couldn't even imagine this. Now it's my reality."
**Avoid:** Toxic positivity, vague platitudes ("believe in yourself").

### 3. Conversational
**Voice:** Friendly, casual, like texting a knowledgeable friend.
**When to use:** Community-building posts, opinion sharing, relatable content.
**Example opening:** "Okay I have to be honest about something I've been doing wrong."
**Avoid:** Over-formal language, corporate-speak.

### 4. Bold
**Voice:** Direct, opinionated, confident — sometimes provocative.
**When to use:** Contrarian takes, industry critique, strong opinions.
**Example opening:** "Unpopular opinion: most productivity advice is making you less productive."
**Avoid:** Arrogance, targeting people (critique ideas not individuals).

### 5. Storytelling
**Voice:** Narrative, personal, journey-based with clear arc.
**When to use:** Case studies, personal experiences, before/after content.
**Example opening:** "6 months ago I almost quit. This is what I learned."
**Avoid:** Burying the point, dragging the story without a clear insight.

### 6. Data-Driven
**Voice:** Precise, evidence-based, analytical.
**When to use:** Research-backed posts, industry stats, performance breakdowns.
**Example opening:** "We analyzed 1,000 Instagram posts. Here's what the data actually shows."
**Avoid:** Numbers without context, overwhelming with stats.

---

## Tone Selection Guide

| Content Type | Recommended Tone |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Tips and how-tos | Educational |
| Personal wins/losses | Inspirational or Storytelling |
| Community engagement | Conversational |
| Hot takes | Bold |
| Case studies | Storytelling or Data-Driven |
| Research/stats posts | Data-Driven |
